The Lattice Model

Service pages operate on four dimensions — each page sits at a specific intersection:

  • Service — SEO, Web Design, Digital Marketing
  • Geography — Cleveland, Columbus, Indianapolis
  • Industry — Roofing, Financial, Healthcare…
  • Specialty — Shopify SEO, Technical SEO, Link Building…

The linking architecture connects them into a lattice, but the anchor text strategy differs by page type:

  • Service pages use descriptive subheadings + clean lists (the heading carries the topical signal)
  • Blog posts deploy contextual, service-qualified anchors within natural sentences
